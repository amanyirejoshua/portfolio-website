Final Year Research · In Progress · Early Stage

ResilientUG

Local-first "Campus Island" for university service continuity

ResilientUG explores how essential university services can remain accessible during external internet uplink failures. The project focuses on a reproducible, warm-standby local infrastructure model for services such as Moodle, library catalogues, and past-paper repositories.

  • Designing automated split-horizon DNS failover with Unbound, Nginx edge caching, and local captive-portal probe handling.
  • Building a single-host fault-injection testbed using Linux network namespaces, nftables, and tc/netem.
  • Measuring Journey Success Rate, Time to Recover, and uplink bandwidth savings across academic workflows.
